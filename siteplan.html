WDD 231 — Individual Website Project

Namcoo Printz

Website Site Plan

01Site Name

Namcoo Printz

  • This name represents a full-service branding and printing company that helps small businesses and individuals bring their print and branding projects to life, from business cards to large-format signage.

Optional domain: namcooprintz.com

02Site Purpose

Namcoo Printz's website serves as an online hub for a local branding and printing business, showcasing available print and design services, business cards, banners, brochures, signage, apparel, and packaging, and allowing potential customers to browse offerings and request a custom quote for their project.

03Scenarios

  1. What printing services does Namcoo Printz offer, and how much do they typically cost?
  2. How long does it take to get business cards or banners printed?
  3. How do I request a custom quote for my print project?

04Color Scheme

Ink Navy #1A1D29 Headings, header/footer background

Paper White #F5F5F0 Page background
Slate #2E3346 Body text, secondary panels
